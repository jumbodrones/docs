The Flight Crew

Flight Crew

Remote Pilot in Command (RPIC)

  • 1
    Ultimate responsibility
    Holds ultimate responsibility for the safe operation of the drone.
  • 2
    Final decision
    Makes the final call to initiate or terminate any flight.
  • 3
    Pre-flight evaluation
    Evaluates all drone activity before operation begins.
  • 4
    Documentation
    Ensures all required documentation is completed.
Note
If the RPIC hasn't signed off, the flight doesn't happen.
Flight Crew

Operator

  • 1
    Designated by the RPIC
    Controls the drone — commonly called "on the sticks."
  • 2
    Visual line of sight
    Maintains Visual Line of Sight (VLOS) with the drone at all times.
  • 3
    Verbal contact
    Maintains verbal contact with the RPIC at all times.
  • 4
    Procedures
    Understands normal, abnormal, and emergency procedures.
Note
The RPIC is often also the Operator — but not in all cases.
Flight Crew

Visual Observer (VO)

  • 1
    Designated by the RPIC
    Assists in flight control to see and avoid hazards aloft or on the ground.
  • 2
    Situational awareness
    Helps maintain awareness through "see-and-avoid."
  • 3
    Verbal contact
    Maintains verbal contact with the RPIC at all times.*
  • 4
    Procedures
    Understands normal, abnormal, and emergency procedures.
Note
*Cell phones are acceptable, with connection confirmed before takeoff and maintained throughout the flight.  However, "daisy-chaining" with cell phones does not satisfy "see and avoid"
Flight Crew

Camera or Payload Operator

  • 1
    Telemetry updates
    Updates the RPIC with critical telemetry information.*
  • 2
    Camera systems
    Responsible for remotely controlling on-board camera systems.
  • 3
    Payload knowledge
    Knowledgeable in operating procedures for specific payloads.
  • 4
    No maneuver authority
    Has no authority to maneuver the aircraft in any unsafe manner.
Note
*Battery percentage, communication signal strength, and any warnings or alerts.
