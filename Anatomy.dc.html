Communication

Two radio links, two jobs

2.4GHz — Transmit (TX)
Carries control input from the pilot's transmitter to the aircraft's radio receiver, letting the pilot fly at a distance.
5.8GHz — Downlink
Sends live video and telemetry back down from the aircraft to the pilot's display, on a separate band to avoid interference.
